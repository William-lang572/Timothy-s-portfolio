WHO AM I?

I’m Isaac Timothy, a Sri Lankan singer-songwriter creating music somewhere between the familiar and the unexpected.

My journey began on The Voice Sri Lanka Season 3, where I reached the finals in 2025. But the real journey began after the show, stepping away from the competition and into a space where I could build something entirely my own.

Drawing from pop, rock, and the sounds I grew up around, I’m exploring what it means to make music that feels modern without losing where it comes from. Each release is another piece of that sound taking shape.

I’m not here to fit into a genre, a formula, or what a Sri Lankan artist is expected to sound like.
I’m here to find out what comes next.
